About Me

A short look at who I am and what I care about.

Education

I am currently studying Cybersecurity at Miva Open University. The programme is helping me understand how digital systems are built, where they become vulnerable, and what practical steps can be taken to protect them.

I come from Oyo State. Studying online has given me the flexibility to keep working and growing other parts of my life while still focusing on my studies.

Why Cybersecurity

Almost everything important now lives online — personal details, business records, conversations, and money. When those systems are weak, real people suffer the consequences.

I chose cybersecurity because I want to understand those risks properly and learn how to reduce them. My goal is to develop practical skills that help keep information safe, make systems harder to break into, and support recovery when something goes wrong.

I am still early in the journey, but the more I learn, the clearer it becomes that this field is about responsibility as much as it is about technology.

Work & Interests

I work in a restaurant. Dealing with customers, managing pressure during busy hours, and paying attention to small details has shaped the way I approach responsibility and teamwork.

Outside of work and studies, I care about fashion, good food, and simple recreation. These things help me stay balanced. I also run a small clothing brand called PAEGIX Clothings, where I explore design ideas and produce limited pieces.

Personal

I am a Christian. My faith influences how I see people, work, and the choices I make daily.

I try to keep life straightforward — study what is useful, do honest work, treat people well, and make space for the things that keep me grounded.
